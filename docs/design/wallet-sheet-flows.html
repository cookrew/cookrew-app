0The constraint S8 already proved, which corrects my earlier spec

🧩
A wallet extension can never reach the canvas browser card Electron has no MV3 action popup, so an injected wallet is simply not there inside an embedded browser — S8 established this from a month of real migration traffic. My earlier flow deck drew an EIP-6963 picker inside the 402 sheet; that is unbuildable as drawn. The wallet lives in the person's real browser or on their phone, and the sheet's job is to hand off cleanly and wait, not to host a connection it cannot make.

S8's three laws carry over intact, and two of them decide states below: a receipt is a probe, not prose (never trust the wallet's success callback — read the chain), and the safe path must be the easiest path (so the QR fallback is always present, never a punishment for not having an extension).

🧾
The receipt line is shown at ENTITLED, and this is the one addition I would fight for "Pay once. Re-downloading it later — new machine, reinstall, update — is free." It has been true since the gate was written and no buyer has ever been told. It belongs at the moment of success, not in a help page: this is the only instant where someone is thinking about what their money bought, and it is the difference between "I rented a download" and "I own this".

Why AWAITING has no primary button

Once the transaction is in the wallet, our sheet can do exactly nothing to advance it — the next event comes from software we do not control. A live-looking PAY button there would be a lie about who is in charge, and pressing it again is how a person double-pays. The sheet becomes a status, and the only control left is the one that was always honest: Cancel, which cancels our waiting and not their transaction. The copy says which.

invalid vs unverifiable — after the money, and this is the case nobody has specified

These two are not wallet errors. They are the review sheet's refusals, and they land after a successful payment — the buyer paid, the download arrived, and verification failed. The right words already exist; what has never been decided is what the sheet says about the money.

CaseWhat the buyer seesWhat it must add after payment
INVALID
…signature_invalid		 This preset doesn't match its signature. It may have been altered since {author} published it. Nothing was installed. "You were charged, and the preset is yours — retrying costs nothing." Entitlement was minted at settlement, so a failed verification is a bad download, not a lost purchase. Without this sentence the buyer reasonably assumes their money is gone, and that assumption is both wrong and the worst possible first impression of a marketplace.
UNVERIFIABLE
…schema_unsupported		 This preset needs a newer Cookrew. It was built for a later preset format, so this version can't check it. Nothing was installed.
Flagging this as a gap in the strings, not a design choice I can make alone. Both blocked strings currently end "Nothing was installed" — true, and dangerously incomplete when money has just moved. I propose one shared additional line, mkt.review.blocked.paid: "You were charged and this preset is yours. Trying again costs nothing." — rendered only when an entitlement exists for the preset that just failed. I have not added it to the module, because whether entitlement really survives a failed verification is Forge's fact to confirm, not mine to assert. If it does not survive, the sentence has to be the opposite one, and that is a refund conversation rather than a copy one.

5Interaction rules carried from the interaction deck

  • Enter is inert on every state of this sheet. Money is the canonical case for the rule: convenience that can charge by muscle memory is not convenience.
  • The scrim is inert once a wallet is connected, and drag-dismiss is disabled on phone for the same reason — a mis-tap must not discard live terms.
  • Focus opens on the dialog, never on PAY. The button is the last tab stop.
  • Nothing blocks the canvas. Agents keep working behind this sheet; it is never a modal spinner over a workspace.
  • No success interstitial between ENTITLED and the review sheet — the receipt line rides the transition rather than costing a click to dismiss.

6Gates

  1. [MAGPIE] No wallet sheet can mount over a terminal card — drive a call to a dry meter and assert an inline notice, not a modal (R5).
  2. [MAGPIE] Opening the dock with an expired token raises no sheet (R24).
  3. [MAGPIE] Enter and scrim-click cannot pay.
  4. [MAGPIE] The receipt line is present in the DOM at ENTITLED — this is the one success-path assertion, and success paths are where copy quietly goes missing.
  5. [FORGE] AWAITING renders no primary control while a transaction is outstanding.
  6. [FORGE] Dismissing at VERIFYING does not cancel settlement; the chip unlocks on its own.
  7. [FORGE] Settlement is confirmed by reading the chain, never by trusting the wallet's callback (S8: a receipt is a probe, not prose).

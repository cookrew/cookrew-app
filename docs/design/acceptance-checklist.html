Two fixtures are deliberately not faked. The six-word fingerprint and the wallet sheet do not exist. The replay — six steps, in the product's own chrome

e2e-demo.html is the contract, and it is more specific than the storyboards: it walks chip → sheet → pay → canvas → update in real chrome. This is the script I execute with real input. The fixture now matches it exactly — Research Crew at v4 with Scout · Digger · Librarian · Editor, and a v5 on the same lineage for step 6.

Give-up #4 now has two death certificates, and I check both. The demo moves the price onto the .cr-chip-price tag, so the cost is visible in the dock, before the first click — earlier than the link. The audit found it missing on the link; it must now be present in both places, and the two must agree. A chip that says $2.50 over a link that says nothing is still a buyer learning the price late.
D-1THE CHIP — cost before the first clickFixture ready
Assertions
  • One slot, one truth. An unowned priced chip shows $2.50; an owned chip shows V4. The slot flips — an owned chip must never show a price, because for an owner the answer to "what would this cost me" is nothing.
  • A free chip shows no price tag at all, not $0.
  • Amber-deep, never rose — computed style, not class name. It is an offer, not a warning; the audit's lesson from B1 is that a colour saying "danger" over a price is copy, and wrong copy.
  • Tapping a gated chip opens the sheet and is never a dead end — and places no node. Control: /api/state node count unchanged.
  • Marketplace is the third chip family, after harness presets and roles.

B-3The owner stays in controlPartly landed
Assertions
  • WHO CAN CALL lists live grants.
  • Revoke stops a call mid-sentence, and says so: "Any call in progress stops."
  • The direction rule — the highest-value copy assertion on this surface. On failure the message must branch on whether the staged change added or removed access. "Access is unchanged" is reassurance when access was being added, and buries the only fact that matters when it was being taken away: "They still have access — revoke didn't complete." Same event, two voices. This is structurally the same defect as collapsing invalid into unverifiable, and it must be gated the same way: construct both directions and assert the strings differ.

Rules this run inherits

  • A wall is a finding, not a failure of mine. Missing surfaces are recorded exactly as a user meets them — a dead click, a raw body, a silent nothing.
  • Verify the surface before judging it. Two traps already cost a wrong reading: a "Not paired" modal that eats every click and is not .cr-reauth, and a remote bundle silently days behind source. Both are asserted before any measurement, and a screenshot is taken — not just a DOM query.
  • Scope every vocabulary scan to product chrome. This canvas renders the specs that define the feature; a document-wide grep finds the document, not the product.
  • Positive controls everywhere. Every "X is refused" runs beside a "Y is accepted" in the same pass, or a surface broken into refusing everything scores perfectly.
  • My own wrong readings go into the report, not into a quiet fix.

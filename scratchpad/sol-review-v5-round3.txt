[P0] Consumer instances are route-isolated but not security-isolated
WHERE: scratchpad/v5-instance-economy.html:113-130,176-179
CLAIM: The two-plane model prevents a consumer token from naming owner agents, but the consumer-controlled agent process still runs under the owner's UID and can reach the owner's host data and control sockets.
EVIDENCE: The design explicitly says worktree + scrub + a separate HERDR_SESSION are accident containment, not a security boundary, and defers containers/separate accounts. A shell-capable instance can read owner transcripts, ~/.cookrew credentials, pairing tokens, SSH/cloud credentials, other worktrees, and same-UID sockets directly; an HTTP 404 does not constrain the child process. That makes the proposed external consumer plane a host-data exfiltration boundary with no enforcement.
FIX: Before untrusted consumer exposure, run each instance in a container or separate OS account with allowlisted mounts, scrubbed env, isolated IPC/herdr sockets, scoped credentials and explicit network policy. Until then, state and enforce a trusted-local-consumer-only product boundary; strong isolation cannot be a paid optional tier.

EVIDENCE: normalize() trims, lowercases and collapses whitespace, so case-sensitive and whitespace-sensitive briefs such as code, shell, YAML or Make recipes can collide.

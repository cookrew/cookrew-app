[P0] A phase cursor is not a write-ahead effect receipt
WHERE: scratchpad/s7-instance-factory-plan.html:53-70,127
CLAIM: The saga does not say whether each cursor is persisted before or after its effect, what evidence identifies a completed effect, or what durability primitive makes the creating row the ownership root; every choice leaves a crash window that can duplicate or lose an effect.
EVIDENCE: Cursor-after-effect repeats mint/workspace/worktree/boot after a crash between effect and append. Cursor-before-effect skips an effect after a crash between append and execution. Some effects are externally visible and not inherently idempotent. Pin a single-writer append log, checksum/schema, file and parent-dir fsync, atomic compaction, fail-closed hydrate, and crash gates at every boundary.

[P0] The 202 credential exchange loses the only bearer after active publication
WHERE: scratchpad/s7-instance-factory-plan.html:54,57-70,79-81,114-118
CLAIM: The effects phase mints a hash while holding the raw token only in memory, then publishes active before a later one-time exchange; a crash or lost response after active strands an accepted credential that neither the caller nor startup reconcile can recover.
EVIDENCE: The raw bearer cannot be reconstructed from its hash. STARTUP RECONCILE is limited to nonterminal rows, so an active-but-unclaimed row does not execute the stated revoke/remint rule.

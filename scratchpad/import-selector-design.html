Design decisions of record

DecisionWhy
Import lives in the dock, as a chip family — not a store page"Add someone's crew" is the same act as "add an agent". One selector, one mental model; the marketplace becomes a place chips come FROM, not a place you go
ADD BY LINK is free and inertAdding ≠ buying. The dock is a shelf; commitment happens at the gate, money at the sheet, connection at placement — three distinct, visible acts
🔒 click opens the gate, never placementVerbatim chipAction: 'place' | 'gate' — the model already shipped for marketplace presets; a locked chip that placed would hide the toll behind the click
The canvas click is the confirmR2, unchanged from every preset. No "are you sure" modal after a gate the user just walked deliberately
One orch card, violet ring + provenance dotThe ruling: the orch is the team's interface. The caller never sees the roster behind it — matching what the server actually exposes
An ended crew dims but staysA vanished chip would gaslight the dock; a dimmed one tells the truth ("no longer serving") and keeps the transcript reachable

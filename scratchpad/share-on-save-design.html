Design decisions of record

DecisionWhy
The share question lives INSIDE the save sheet, not a second sheet after itOne breath, one act. A follow-up sheet is a place to abandon; a section is a place to glance past. Default answers it safely.
Primary renames to SAVE · START SERVING when a public option is pickedThe button says everything the click does. A plain SAVE that also published would be the quiet-consequence bug this product refuses everywhere.
The door fact (mkt.serve.door) appears only when a public option is selectedIt is the answer to the fear that appears at that moment ("which of my agents are exposed?"). Before that it is noise.
Sessions table lives on the served team's card (state C), not a global panel"Who is on?" is a question about a thing you published. Attaching it to the thing is what lets WHO CAN CALL die.
The shelf chip shows TAKING CALLS · n, greenThe standing state must be visible at rest — a team quietly serving with no indicator is how an owner forgets they opened a door.
Price is per SESSION in M1Matches the instantiator's unit (a session is the thing minted); per-call metering (R12) layers on later without changing this sheet.

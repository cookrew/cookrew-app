The Instantiator R30

First gated call → a live WorkspaceSession from the template at its pin, in a sandbox, routed to its Conductor. A composer of machinery that ships — not new subsystems. · branches feat/instantiator-slice1..2

The one path

The pin is resolved once, at mint. A running session keeps the version it started on; only a new one gets the latest — which is what makes ana-1 on V1 and ana-2 on V2 a fact of the data, not a race. END reuses the revoke seam (CallsInFlight.cancelWhere): it cuts the running call before it deletes the sandbox, because a stop that races a live agent is a crash.

What confinement does NOT buy, stated rather than discovered: it constrains where an agent starts, not an OS boundary. The honest claim is "a caller's files land in their folder," not "a caller cannot read yours" — and the R30 privacy ruling already narrowed the promise to "Cookrew never sends your conversation anywhere else."

[P1] The async fold still contains unbounded synchronous main-thread regions
WHERE: src/main/turn-store.ts:62-73,949-995,1008-1037; tests/turn-store-fold-async.test.ts:88-154; scratchpad/v5-export-concurrency.html:I6
CLAIM: Yielding per 256 KiB read and per 200 records does not bound work by bytes or move filesystem durability off Electron main. One oversized JSONL record and the final fsync can still monopolize the event loop.
EVIDENCE: readRecordsChunked repeatedly Buffer.concat's an unterminated carry, then decodes and JSON.parse's the entire line synchronously. replaceChunked synchronously JSON.stringify's 200 records and Buffer.from's/writes that body regardless of their byte size, then calls fsyncSync on the whole temp. FIX: Run fold parsing/serialization/durable I/O in a worker or genuinely asynchronous storage path, byte-bound every unit rather than record-count-bound it, and add a fixture with one very large record plus a delayed fsync. Gate maximum event-loop delay through commit, not merely the presence of setImmediate yields.
